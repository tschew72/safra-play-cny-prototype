Design System Summary — “The Ascent” · v1.0 · September 2026

01 · Concept

The Ascent

The mountain goat is the season’s hero: it climbs terraced golden peaks the way players climb the leaderboard. Campaigns are ascents, points are altitude, and the season finale is the Golden Peak. The theme keeps Chinese New Year warmth — vermilion, gold, plum blossom — but lightened onto rice-paper cream with a fresh jade counter-accent, so the interface feels festive yet calm and breathable.

Voice: warm, encouraging, unhurried. Motifs: paper-cut goat, terraced peaks, auspicious clouds, plum blossom, lanterns, red seal stamps with blessing characters (羊年大吉 · 恭喜发财 · 喜气洋洋 · 新年如意). Texture: subtle paper grain, hairline gold borders, soft shadows — never glossy, never neon.
